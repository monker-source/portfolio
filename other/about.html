Sasha Smirnov — graphic designer specializing in typography and lettering.

He designs logotypes and custom lettering — from restrained, editorial marks to bold, high-impact identities — and works on typefaces in both Cyrillic and Latin scripts.

He graduated as a book designer from the HSE Art and Design School and, in 2025, completed the Expert Class in Type Design at the Plantin Institute for Typography.

Freelancing since 2021, Sasha has collaborated with design agencies, publishing houses, and IT companies, including ABC Dinamo, Blaze Type, Tuman Agency and Playgama

Sasha provides following services
Custom typeface design
Custom letterings and logos
Customisation of typeface
Typeface revivals from source
