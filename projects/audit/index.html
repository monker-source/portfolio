Yandex Audit is a division responsible for combating fraud and ensuring compliance. The logo's letterforms are built from continuous strokes interrupted by small cuts — as if the surface were sliced open to reveal the mechanism underneath. This reflects the core idea of an audit: opening up a system to examine how it actually works.

The handwritten character of the typeface evokes margin notes and field observations — the small, manual marks of someone documenting anomalies in real time. It reinforces the idea that auditing isn't a one-off check, but a living, ongoing process.
