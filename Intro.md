SASHA is a designer specializing in logotypes and custom lettering — from restrained wordmark to bold, high-impact letters. He works on typefaces in both Cyrillic and Latin scripts. He graduated as a book designer from the HSE Art and Design School and, in 2025, completed the Expert Class in Type Design at the Plantin Institute for Typography. Freelancing since 2021, Sasha has collaborated with design agencies, publishing houses, and IT companies. Among his clients are ABC Dinamo, Blaze Type, Tuman Agency and Playgama.
