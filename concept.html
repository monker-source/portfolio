About

I trained as a graphic designer and drifted into type the way most of us do — by getting picky about kerning and never coming back. My work sits between craft and utility: typefaces that hold up in a paragraph and still have something to say at 200 points.

I take on custom commissions and license a small retail library. If you're building something that needs its own letterforms, I'd like to hear about it.

Currently drawing: a low-contrast serif for a news client.

Reading: specimen books from the 1960s, mostly for the marginalia.
